# Safety Boundaries

Persona Generator is limited to fictional, low-stakes, advisory use.

## Prohibited

- Real-person covert profiling.
- Diagnosis or clinical claims.
- Psychometric scoring.
- Sensitive-attribute inference.
- High-stakes suitability judgments.
- Hiring screening.
- Credit or insurance use.
- Legal or medical decisions.
- Educational placement.
- Surveillance.
- Manipulation.
- Autonomous final decisions.
- Unsupported academic claims.
- Static persona catalog expansion.
- Real-user readiness claims.

## Required

- Label scenarios as fictional.
- Keep execution bounded. Persist only explicitly confirmed profile revisions and assignments; stored state does not authorize ongoing activity.
- Preserve uncertainty and dissent.
- Use manual review.
- Stop when the bounded task is complete.
- Treat all outputs as advisory.
