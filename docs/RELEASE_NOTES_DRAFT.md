## Recorded v1 pilot evidence

The [2026-10-22 engineering pilot](../evaluation/pilot-2026-10-22/REPORT.md) retained 102 synthetic task responses in 76 fresh contexts under its frozen v1 instructions. It records mixed, task-specific instruction-following observations, including 28 G09 budget violations, three unclear guardrail ratings and non-contrasts. Its raw outputs, frozen rubric, locked ratings and failure classifications remain unchanged. Each item had one judge; the ratings do not establish inter-judge reliability. The [independent review](../evaluation/pilot-2026-10-22/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) preserves the B01 threshold inconsistency rather than rerating it into a success.

These are actual retained model observations, but the pilot used synthetic instructions and did not complete the production interview/confirmation workflow. It does not demonstrate human personality, psychological construct implementation, general reliability, universal portability or readiness for real-user use. It does not satisfy the original full [behavior protocol](../validation/BEHAVIOR_PROTOCOL.md).

## V2 design work only

The candidate adds five proposed revised mappings: B03 `map.compassion.v2`, B05 `map.industriousness.v2`, B06 `map.orderliness.v2`, B08 `map.assertiveness.v2` and B10 `map.openness.v2`. [Mapping cards](../persona-design-v0.2/mapping-v2/cards.json) expose exact instruction clauses and observable features. Adherence, contrasts across conditions, task usefulness and safety are separate questions; overlapping features are not forbidden simply because an anchor does not require them. B10 narrows its scope to requested feasible-option breadth, without an originality or human Openness claim.

The separate [Markdown workflow](../persona-design-v0.2/mapping-v2/WORKFLOW.md) starts all ten aspects unspecified and traces choices or free text through answer IDs, explicit mapping/rule approval, a complete preview revision, authentic owner confirmation, amendment invalidation, new confirmation and fresh-session reuse. Its examples are **handwritten illustrations**, not model outputs or authentic owner approvals. The v2 contract is independently proposed; existing v1 validator results do not establish v2 contract acceptance.

The five remaining mappings retain their `.v1` IDs only through explicit opt-in. B01/B02 remain experimental expression controls. There is no automatic spec upgrade, mapping approval transfer or v1 evidence transfer to revised instructions. Human source definitions remain vocabulary provenance, not behavioral validation; see the [source ledger](../persona-design-v0.2/sources.md) and [claims matrix](CLAIMS_MATRIX.md).
