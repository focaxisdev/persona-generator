## Review decision

No missing raw output, unresolved invariant failure, synthetic confirmation, handwritten example, or unelicited contrast may be counted as a pass. Any future scoped acceptance requires a prospective decision naming exact supported behavior, versions, cases and exclusions. Failures can motivate a versioned redesign; they cannot be silently removed or rerated into success. No public merge, release or deployment occurs as part of this integration.
