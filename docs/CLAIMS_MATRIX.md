## Historical observations stay historical

The [v1 engineering pilot](../evaluation/pilot-2026-10-22/REPORT.md) retained 102 model-generated outputs on synthetic tasks in 76 fresh contexts with mixed, task-specific instruction-following observations, 28 G09 budget violations and three unclear guardrail ratings. The [independent review](../evaluation/pilot-2026-10-22/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) records a B01 threshold inconsistency; locked ratings remain unchanged. Five initial pairs were conservative candidate exact-anchor observations, three with confidence limitations; none establishes psychological validity or ten-trait implementation. Single-judge ratings do not establish inter-judge reliability. Refer to the report for original categories, attempts, model settings and missing coverage.

The pilot did not complete the production interview/owner-confirmation workflow or the full original [behavior protocol](../validation/BEHAVIOR_PROTOCOL.md). No result transfers to the new operation IDs. Historical B01/B02 remain experimental expression proxies (weak wording controls), never implementations of full human Volatility or Withdrawal. Frozen [mapping-v2](../persona-design-v0.2/mapping-v2/README.md) and its planned 50-response diagnostic remain NOT RUN historical design, not the core runtime or release gate. No retrospective rerating, budget reclassification, hidden gate change or automatic migration is introduced.
