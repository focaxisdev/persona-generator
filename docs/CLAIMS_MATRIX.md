## Actual mapping scope and bounded observations

The [source ledger](../persona-design-v0.2/sources.md) supports attributed **human definitions**. The following behavior translations are **proposed engineering mappings**, not findings of that human research. The pilot's locked categorical model ratings are **bounded observations**, not psychological validation. S1/S2/S3 are three shared fictional paper-activity tasks. [Independent model review](../evaluation/pilot-2026-10-22/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) is complete; its rating ambiguities remain visible. Coordinator acceptance is pending.

For conservative description of initial pairs, exact anchor attainment requires both the low and high target categories, usable task responses, and no rated violation or unresolved relevant guardrail. An intermediate category does not attain a high or low anchor. On the present ratings, only B02 S1/S3, B07 S1, and B09 S1/S3 are candidate pairs under that narrow reading. Three of these five pairs have a limited-confidence target rating (B07 S1 high, B09 S1 high, B09 S3 low); only B02 S1/S3 have clear target ratings on both sides. These are candidate observations, not validated successes. This is not a new release threshold or a substitute for the frozen plan. Other categorical differences remain visible without being described as full anchor attainment.
