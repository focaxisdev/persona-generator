## v0.2 Candidate Evidence Boundary

The new [named registry](../persona-design-v0.2/construct-registry.md) and interview are a separate increment. Their sources support human construct names and definitions, not agent validity. Static fixture checks test document contracts; hand-authored examples are not model observations.

**Bounded engineering pilot recorded; full behavior protocol NOT COMPLETED.** The [pilot report](../evaluation/pilot-2026-10-22/REPORT.md) documents 102 genuine synthetic responses in 76 fresh contexts: 60 initial low/high answers across ten mappings and three tasks, three shared neutral baselines, 26 same-context followups, and 13 fresh repeats/reloads. Separate model contexts rated anonymized responses using a frozen rubric. This is model assessment, not independent human review; independent semantic review and coordinator acceptance remain pending.

Human-source definitions, proposed engineering mappings, and actual model observations are separate evidence classes. The observed categories support only narrow instruction-following descriptions for the recorded requests and tasks. Conservative exact-anchor reading identifies candidate pairs B02 S1/S3 (downside placement), B07 S1 (opening expression), and B09 S1/S3 (reasoning route), with no rated violations on those pairs. B07 S1 high, B09 S1 high and B09 S3 low have limited-confidence target ratings; only B02 S1/S3 have clear target ratings on both sides. These are narrow observations, not validated successes. Other differences include intermediate anchors, absent contrasts and failures; see the [ten-mapping scope table](CLAIMS_MATRIX.md#actual-mapping-scope-and-bounded-observations) and [complete categorical tables](../evaluation/pilot-2026-10-22/analysis/report-tables.md). B01/B02 remain experimental expression controls only; B04/B07 concern wording, B05/B06 overlap in planning structure, and B10 tests narrow option breadth.

All **28/102 G09 budget violations (251–266 whitespace-delimited words; 18 initial, six followup, four reload)** and **three unclear guardrail ratings** remain visible. Unclear ratings concern task adequacy (G01), unestablished larger-sheet availability (G02), and preservation of the requested comparison (G06). No other locked guardrail violations were recorded; this is not a global safety pass. A matching target category cannot erase a failure. The original [behavior protocol](../validation/BEHAVIOR_PROTOCOL.md) still requires original case tasks, all five conditions, independent fresh repeats, full multiround/reload coverage, authentic preview confirmation and manual review. The reduced synthetic design does not satisfy it; release remains **BLOCKED**. See [release acceptance](RELEASE_ACCEPTANCE.md).

The pilot retains exact packets, hashes, dispatch records, locked ratings and all 102 authorized public [raw outputs](../evaluation/pilot-2026-10-22/raw/). They are experimental evidence, not an end-user-confirmed spec, runtime persona inventory or hand-authored fixture. Model backend snapshot and unexposed settings remain unknown. There are no population estimates, broad reliability findings, ten implemented traits, psychological validity or cross-model stability claims.

The legacy statements below are inherited v0.1 curated claims. Their private raw evidence was not independently re-audited for this increment, and they cannot count as v0.2 evidence.

## Inherited v0.1 Bounded Evidence

- Persona Pack v0.1 was exercised across contrasting fictional work-context examples without schema expansion.
- Restricted-context and fresh-session consumption trials support bounded Markdown portability claims.
- One bounded on-demand fictional persona instantiation dry-run showed that a capable agent can generate a minimum useful set of differentiated ephemeral fictional personas for one low-stakes scenario.
- One bounded panel rehearsal showed reviewed ephemeral fictional personas can produce differentiated advisory contributions and a dissent-preserving synthesis for one low-stakes scenario.
- One bounded serialization and reload trial showed that one selected on-demand fictional persona can be serialized into Persona Pack v0.1 and consumed from exact files in a fresh session.

## What This Does Not Prove

- No universal reliability claim.
- No universal panel-quality claim.
- No cross-provider portability claim.
- No psychometric-validity claim.
- No real-user-readiness claim.
- No high-stakes suitability claim.
- No autonomous final-decision claim.
