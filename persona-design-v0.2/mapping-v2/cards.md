# Proposed mapping cards v2

Status: **proposal only; no v2 behavioral evidence; not an end-user-confirmed spec**. The machine-readable source is [cards.json](cards.json). These five new versions supersede no historical evidence. They are investigator-authored instructions for fictional, low-stakes output design, not predefined personas, personality scores or psychological validation.

The [existing registry](../construct-registry.md) remains the source for human construct definitions, aspect assignments and human-source attribution. Each `map.*.v2` below is a new project mapping proposal, not a claim supported by those human sources. Local provenance ID `src.project-mapping-v2` resolves to this proposal's `cards.json`; it denotes original engineering authorship only. The existing `src.project-mapping-v0.2` ledger entry applies only to retained `.v1` mappings and does not support revised v2 rule text. Registry v1 anchors and the pilot's frozen rubric, raw outputs, ratings and reports remain unchanged. Version 2 has **no observed outputs or outcomes**. Other mappings, especially B01/B02's experimental expression controls, are outside this diagnostic.

| New mapping / axis | Observable dimension | Low | Middle | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `map.compassion.v2` / F03 | Stated need in accommodation generation | State the need and provide a usable accommodation. | Also compare an accommodation with another viable approach on the stated need. | Use that need to organize generation and comparison of at least two usable accommodations. |

| `map.openness.v2` / F10 | Narrow feasible-option breadth | Exactly one materially distinct usable approach. | Exactly two. | Exactly three. |

These summaries do not add requirements. Exact anchor instruction bytes are rendered by joining the referenced `rule_clauses[].text` values with a single newline in anchor reference order. Each feature has its own clause trace; each task requirement has a stable task requirement ID. No rubric requirement may be silently introduced. Common task requirements, safety, accuracy, scope and human authority remain equal across conditions.

`low`, `middle`, and `high` are qualitative instruction labels. `unspecified` is explicitly unconstrained, never a midpoint. Neutral has no persona packet. In each targeted condition the other nine B01–B10 aspects remain unspecified; in unspecified all ten remain unspecified. B03 low may compare; B06 low may use stages; B08 low may recommend and may choose an advisory option when requested. Co-occurrence is expected and must remain visible, rather than being forced into exclusive categories. No anchor authorizes reduced usefulness, missing checks or extra execution.

Every feature is rated separately as present, absent or unclear before labels are revealed. B10's exact-count predicates describe observable cardinality; judges also retain total proposed approach count and developed/usable/feasible approach count and the reasoning for ambiguous grouping. They are not instructed to choose exactly one categorical label: zero feasible approaches, extra undeveloped or infeasible candidates, more than three, or ambiguity may yield no supported count predicate. Exactly N requires both total and feasible counts to equal N; three feasible plus one infeasible candidate cannot pass exactly three. Familiarity is not part of any B10 rule or rating. The same 250-word response ceiling applies to all conditions.

B05 checkpoint recognition concerns at least one intermediate completed outcome, not ordering. The final stopping scope is common task work. B06 concerns step ordering, dependencies, required checks and adaptation, not completion recognition. F05/F06 co-occurrence is recorded, not treated as evidence of independent mechanisms. B06 precedence and feasibility with initial table unavailability are common mandatory facts; explicit dependency explanation and conditional resequencing are separately observed features. Baseline saturation remains possible. B03 has a task that permits comparisons without requiring every answer to compare. B08 changes advisory initiative without granting decision authority. B10 tests only literal breadth instructions, not originality, imagination, usefulness, artistry or human Openness.

The [diagnostic plan](../../evaluation/diagnostic-v2/PLAN.md) specifies a finite proposed comparison. A supported instruction contrast would remain specific to these exact tasks, models and conditions. Five different focused tasks confound between-task comparisons; they cannot establish construct separation. This proposal does not complete the original full protocol or unblock release.

The trace for each card is human definition/source in the unchanged registry → new `map.*.v2` clause IDs → focused task T03/T05/T06/T08/T10 and its common requirement IDs → label-free F03/F05/F06/F08/F10 predicates. Expected features reference exact clauses included in the assigned anchor; common rubric requirements reference exact task requirement IDs. This is task-bound reuse of vocabulary, not evidence that a general human trait has been implemented.
