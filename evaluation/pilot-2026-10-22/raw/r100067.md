Assuming shapes can be moved and pencil details are allowed, these approaches use the same twenty minutes and available materials:

- **Town:** Use rectangles as buildings and circles as trees or wheels. Familiar outlines may help recognition, though crowded buildings could remain confusing.
- **Garden:** Use circles as flower heads and rectangles as beds or a path, with pencil stems. Repetition may look clear, though flowers might need details to distinguish them from other circles.
- **Compare both:** Spend five minutes trying the alternative layout. This offers direct evidence but leaves five fewer minutes for refinement.
