Welcome—there’s room to make a small, playable draft together.

The first draft has been described as confusing; the unclear steps still need identifying. Twelve slogans on one small card may be difficult to read. I’d suggest three playing cards, with brief rules and a written example on scrap paper. The participant’s request makes that example useful; they could read it before a practice turn and point out any missing steps.

Two feasible options:

- **Matching:** Draw two circles and one triangle on three cards. Shuffle them face down; a player flips two, earning one point for matching circles and zero otherwise. Reshuffle after each turn. Give everyone three turns. This is simple but mostly chance.
- **Sorting:** Draw a circle, triangle, and square on three cards. Write a target order on paper. Players arrange the shuffled cards to match it, earning one point for a correct arrangement. This offers a visible checking task, though it may be very easy.

A written matching example could say: “Alex flips a circle and a triangle: zero points. Turn all cards face down and shuffle for the next player.”
