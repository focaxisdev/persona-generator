Two feasible approaches:

- **Garden:** Circles could become flower heads, with rectangles as beds or a path. This could be simple to arrange, though flowers may need pencil stems to read clearly.

**Written example:** “Garden: place four circles above a rectangle bed. Draw stems connecting them to the bed. Use two rectangles for a path; arrange the remaining pieces as a bench and two shrubs.”
