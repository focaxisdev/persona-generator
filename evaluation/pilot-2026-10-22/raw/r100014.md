The confusing first draft is useful feedback; we could ask which part is unclear before choosing a fix. For the participant who finds fast explanations difficult, a short written guide beside the collage could offer more time to follow along.

- **Garden:** circles could become flower heads and rectangles could become beds or paths, with pencil details connecting them. This might also read clearly, but that remains untested.

One usable written example: “Town: place three rectangle buildings along a penciled road. Put two circle trees beside them. Add doors and trunks.” Keep this guide beside the scene; consider one large title if needed.
