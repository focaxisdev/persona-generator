# Scenario Brief

## Fixture Status

This is fictional public fixture material. It does not describe real people, a real organization, or a real event.

## Scenario

A fictional neighborhood volunteer group wants to host a limited public beta of a knowledge-sharing night. The event will invite a small group of community members to share practical skills such as repairing household items, reading local transit maps, preparing simple budget meals, and using a public library catalog.

The organizers want to publish a simple sign-up page and a short facilitator guide. Before publishing, they want temporary fictional perspectives to review clarity, accessibility, risk, and volunteer coordination.

## Decision or Rehearsal Question

What should the organizers change before publishing the sign-up page and facilitator guide?

## Why This Is Fictional and Low-Stakes

- All people, roles, and event details are invented.
- No real volunteer data is included.
- The decision is reversible.
- The output is advisory only.
- No eligibility, medical, legal, financial, hiring, surveillance, or education-placement decision is involved.

## Desired Output

- three ephemeral fictional personas
- manual review status for each persona
- one bounded advisory synthesis
- visible dissent and minority-view preservation
- explicit stop

## Prohibited Use

Do not use this fixture for real-person profiling, diagnosis, psychometric scoring, protected or sensitive attribute inference, covert profiling, static persona catalog construction, or high-stakes decisions.
