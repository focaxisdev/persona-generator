# Limitations

## Fixture Limits

- This example is fictional.
- It is for public documentation only.
- It does not describe real people, real volunteers, a real venue, or a real organization.
- It is not a reusable persona preset.
- It is not runtime inventory.
- It is not a dataset.
- It is not a static persona catalog.

## Evidence Limits

- Construct anchors are qualitative and scenario-local.
- Source-note status is `source-gap` in the sample persona traces.
- The sample does not prove general reliability.
- The sample does not prove panel quality.
- The sample does not prove cross-provider portability.
- The sample does not prove psychometric validity.
- The sample does not prove real-user readiness.

## Safety Limits

Do not use this example for:

- real-person profiling
- diagnosis
- psychometric scoring
- protected or sensitive attribute inference
- covert profiling
- hiring, legal, medical, credit, insurance, surveillance, education placement, policing, immigration, or eligibility decisions
- manipulation or exploitative persuasion
- autonomous final decisions

## Stop Rule

The fixture stops after a bounded advisory synthesis. Additional personas, panel rounds, persistence, or handoff packages require a separate explicit task.
