## Prohibited Uses

This Persona Pack must not be used for:

- diagnosis or clinical assessment
- psychometric scoring
- objective personality truth claims
- hidden motive claims
- protected or sensitive attribute inference
- honesty, deception, morality, trustworthiness, or suitability scoring
- hiring, employment, credit, insurance, legal, medical, education placement, policing, immigration, surveillance, or eligibility decisions
- manipulation or exploitative persuasion
- automatic final decisions

## Interpretation Rules

- Treat claims as evidence-bounded operational summaries.
- Keep confidence labels qualitative.
- Preserve contradictions and unknowns.
- Do not infer beyond the corpus scope.
- Do not treat the pack as a complete representation of a person.
- Do not use the pack without reading evidence, unknowns, contradictions, and boundaries.

## Stop Conditions

Stop if:

- required source status is missing
- source consent or handling status is unclear
- evidence cannot be traced
- unsupported claims are requested
- high-stakes use is requested
- a database, API, app, embeddings, or hidden prompt state becomes necessary to understand the pack
