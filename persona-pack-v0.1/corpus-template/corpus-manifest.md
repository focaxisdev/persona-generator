## Prohibited Uses

The corpus and resulting Persona Pack must not be used for:

- diagnosis or clinical assessment
- psychometric scoring
- hidden-trait truth claims
- real-person profiling without explicit scope and consent
- protected or sensitive attribute inference
- honesty, deception, morality, trustworthiness, or suitability scoring
- hiring, legal, medical, credit, insurance, education placement, policing, immigration, surveillance, or eligibility decisions
- manipulation or exploitative persuasion
